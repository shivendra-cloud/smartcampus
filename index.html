Impact of IT Infrastructure on Academic Institution Administration

A field project survey on how IT systems shape administrative efficiency, communication, and decision-making in academic institutions.

About Methodology Findings Recommendations Team

About the Project

This project examines the perceived impact of IT infrastructure on academic administration through a 20-question survey distributed to administrators, faculty, and staff.

Methodology

An online questionnaire with Likert-scale responses was circulated. Over 50 responses were collected and analysed using Google Sheets.

Recommendations
